# Design benchmark: the best similar platforms

Prepared 24 November 2026 for SBF Studio. Purpose: keep the current look (which the owner likes) and borrow what the best platforms do well.

## Who we compared against

| Platform | Known for | What SBF Studio borrows |
|---|---|---|
| Later | Visual-first, Instagram-focused planning, media library, link in bio | A visual feed preview |
| Planoly | Drag-and-drop Instagram grid planner | **Instagram grid preview** of upcoming and live posts |
| Buffer | Simplicity: a calm queue, very little chrome | Quiet notices, one clear action per screen (already done) |
| Sprout Social | Reporting, unified inbox | Analytics on the Overview, inbox (already done) |
| Canva | Templates, friendly visual tiles, AI generation | Design studio, picture-style tiles (already done) |
| Linear / Notion | Polish: Cmd+K command menu, keyboard-first, progressive disclosure, fast perceived loading | **Command menu (Ctrl+K)**, **loading skeletons**, single-open cards |

## What the leaders have in common

1. **A command menu (Ctrl/Cmd+K).** Now expected in any product with more than about ten areas. One box to go anywhere, do anything and search everything, with shortcuts shown beside each command.
2. **Seeing the feed before it's live.** Planoly and Later both let you see how the next posts will sit on the Instagram grid. This is the feature creators cite most.
3. **Progressive disclosure.** Show the essentials, reveal detail on demand (the editor's single-open cards already do this).
4. **Fast-feeling pages.** Skeleton placeholders while a page loads, so nothing jumps.
5. **Consistent page headers and kind empty states.** Every screen opens the same way; empty screens explain what goes there and offer the next step.

## What we changed (keeping the current design)

- Command menu: press Ctrl+K (or click "Quick find" in the top bar) to jump to any page, start any action, or search.
- Instagram grid preview in the planner: a 3-across profile view of scheduled, approved and published Instagram posts using their designs.
- Every page header now matches the Overview: small label, medium-weight serif title.
- Empty states use the brand arch and heart, with a clear next step.
- Loading skeletons in brand tints while pages load.
